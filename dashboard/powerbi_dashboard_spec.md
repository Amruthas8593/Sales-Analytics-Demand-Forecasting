# Power BI Dashboard — Sales Performance & Key Business Metrics

## Dashboard Purpose

Interactive Power BI reporting for dairy sales performance, inventory patterns, pricing strategies, and demand insights.

## Page 1 — Executive Sales Performance

KPI cards:
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Average Transaction Value

Visuals:
- Monthly revenue trend
- Monthly units sold trend
- Revenue by category
- Profit by category

## Page 2 — Product & Inventory Analysis

Visuals:
- Product revenue ranking
- Product demand / units sold
- High-demand product table
- Inventory-priority view

Slicers:
- Product
- Category
- Date

## Page 3 — Regional & Channel Performance

Visuals:
- Revenue by region
- Profit by region
- Units sold by region
- Revenue by sales channel
- Profit by sales channel

Slicers:
- Region
- Channel
- Customer type

## Page 4 — Pricing Strategy

Visuals:
- Unit price vs units sold
- Discount percentage vs profit
- Revenue by discount band
- Profit margin by product

## Power BI Interactivity

- Date slicer
- Product/category slicers
- Region/channel slicers
- Cross-filtering between visuals
- Drill-through from category to product detail
- Tooltips for KPI and product context

## Business Questions Answered

- Which products drive sales and profit?
- Which regions and channels perform best?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which products show high demand and require inventory priority?
- How do discounts affect revenue and profit?
